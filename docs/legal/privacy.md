# CommitCV Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 19, 2026
**Last updated:** November 19, 2026

This policy explains what information CommitCV collects when you use the official website at [commitcv.com](https://commitcv.com) (the "Service"), where that information lives, and the choices you have. It replaces the earlier draft policy dated September 6, 2025.

## The short version

- **Your resume stays on your device.** Resumes are written, compiled and downloaded in your browser. We never receive your resume content and cannot read it.
- **We collect anonymous usage statistics**, such as how many resumes are created or downloaded. These never include anything you type into your resume.
- **We do not sell your data.** There are no user accounts.
- **Clearing your browser data deletes your resumes.** We keep no copy, so we cannot recover them.

## 1. Who we are

CommitCV is operated by a group of maintainers ("CommitCV", "we", "us"). You can reach us at **contact@commitcv.com**.

CommitCV is open-source software. This policy applies only to the official instance at commitcv.com. It does not apply to forks, self-hosted copies, or other websites built from our code, which are run by other people and are not under our control.

## 2. Where your information lives

| Where                                        | What is there                                                                 | Who can access it                       |
| -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **Your browser** (local storage and cookies) | Your resume content, drafts, settings, and the cookies described in section 5 | You. This data is not sent to us.       |
| **Your device**                              | PDFs and other files you download                                             | You                                     |
| **Our server**                               | Anonymous analytics (section 4.1) and standard server logs (section 4.2)      | CommitCV maintainers                    |

## 3. What we do not collect

We do not collect, receive or store:

- The content of your resumes, including your name, contact details, work history, education, skills or any other text you enter
- Your files or PDFs
- Passwords (there are no accounts)
- Payment information (the Service is currently free)

The Typst compiler that turns your resume into a PDF runs inside your browser. Your resume is not uploaded for compilation.

## 4. What we do collect

### 4.1 Anonymous analytics

To understand how CommitCV is used and to fix problems, we collect anonymous events and technical information.

**Usage events (counts only, never content):**

- A resume was created
- A resume was downloaded as a PDF
- A resume was deleted
- A resume was imported or exported

**Technical context:**

- Pages viewed
- Browser and operating system
- Device type and screen size
- Country (derived from your IP address at the time of the request; the IP address itself is not stored with the analytics event)
- Referrer (the site that sent you to CommitCV)
- Language setting

**Errors and performance:**

- Resume compile errors (the type of error only, never your resume text)
- Crash and JavaScript error reports
- Page load performance

**Anonymous visitor ID:** a random identifier stored in your browser lets us count unique visitors. It is not linked to your name, email or resume content. You can reset it at any time by clearing your cookies and site data.

**Our rule:** no resume content, names, email addresses or other personal details you enter are ever placed in an analytics event.

We do not store full IP addresses in our analytics data, and we do not use analytics to build profiles of individual people.

**How analytics are collected:** we use [Umami](https://umami.is), an open-source analytics tool that we host ourselves on our own server. Analytics data is never sent to a third-party analytics provider. Umami does not store your full IP address. The IP address is used only to work out your approximate country and to tell visits apart, and is then discarded. Our own code sends the usage events listed above (for example, "a resume was created") to Umami, and these events contain only the event name, never anything from your resume.

**Unique visitor counting:** to count unique visitors, our analytics tool creates an anonymous identifier on our server from technical details of your visit. This identifier is not stored in your browser, is not linked to your name, email or resume content, and cannot be used to identify you.

### 4.2 Server logs

Like most websites, our server automatically records basic technical information about each request: IP address, date and time, the page or file requested, the response status, and your browser's user-agent string. We use these logs to keep the Service running, fix problems and protect against abuse. Logs are kept for up to 30 days and are not used to identify you or to look at your resumes.

## 5. Cookies and browser storage

We use cookies and your browser's local storage for the following purposes:

- **Keeping your work.** Your resume content is saved in your browser's local storage so it is still there when you come back. A cookie is used so your editing session is not lost if you reload or revisit the page. These cookies hold only a session identifier and other non-personal technical information. They never contain your resume content or any other personal information, and your resume content is not sent to our servers.
- **Settings.** Preferences you choose, such as language.
- **Anonymous visitor ID.** Described in section 4.1.

You can view or delete cookies and local storage at any time in your browser's settings. If you do, your saved resumes will be deleted and cannot be recovered, so download or export anything you want to keep first.

We will not send analytics data to a third-party analytics provider without first updating this policy.

## 7. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

- Provide and maintain the Service
- Understand usage and decide what to improve
- Find and fix bugs and performance problems
- Keep the Service secure and prevent abuse
- Meet legal obligations

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

## 8. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We do not share information with anyone except:

- **When required by law**, such as a valid legal request
- **To protect** the Service, our users or others from fraud, abuse or security threats

Anonymous, aggregate statistics (for example, "resumes created this month") may be published, for instance in project updates.

## 9. How long we keep information

| Information                          | Retention                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Resumes and settings in your browser | Until you delete them or clear your browser data. We do not control this.      |
| Server logs                          | Up to 30 days                                                                  |
| Anonymous analytics                  | Up to 24 months, after which they are deleted or kept only as aggregate totals |

## 10. Your choices and rights

Because your resume lives only in your browser, you are in control of it: you can edit, export or delete it at any time, and clearing your browser's site data removes it entirely.

We hold very little information that can be linked to you. If you believe we hold personal information about you and want to access, correct or delete it, email **privacy@commitcv.com** and we will respond within a reasonable time. We may need some details from you (such as the approximate date of your visit) to find anything, since we do not keep accounts.

Our practices are intended to align with Canadian privacy law, including the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA. We do not sell personal information, and the limited analytics we collect are based on our legitimate interest in running and improving a free service. Contact us to exercise any rights you have.

## 11. Security

Your resume content never leaves your device, which is the strongest protection we can offer for it. The Service is delivered over HTTPS, and we limit access to our server and logs to the maintainers who need it. No system is perfectly secure, so please protect your own device and keep a backup of anything important.

## 12. Where data is processed

Our server and analytics are operated from Canada. If you use the Service from elsewhere, the limited information described in section 4 may be processed in Canada.

## 13. Children

The Service is not directed at young children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since we do not receive resume content or run accounts, we hold nothing that identifies a child. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact contact@commitcv.com.

## 14. Future changes to the Service

CommitCV is free today. We may add new features in the future, such as new integrations, donations, advertising or paid features. If a new feature changes what information is collected or how it is handled, we will update this policy and record the change in the [legal changelog](legal-changelog.md) before the feature launches. We will not sell your personal information.

The "Last updated" date at the top shows the most recent change. Continuing to use the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.
